The Rules

  1. No griefing: Any pranks should take less than 5 minutes to clean up.
  2. Invites: Anyone is welcome on the server, just make sure you whitelist them. I'd prefer they are in the Discord so they get the messages when mods are added, but if you're willing to be the in-between, then that's fine.
  3. Be respectful: Just be adults, I believe in you!
  4. Have fun: And kill the Endermen!

Backup Schedule

The server runs regular backups to keep our progress safe. The times below have been automatically converted to your local timezone.

  • Lite Backups: Every 6 hours starting at Midnight (Chicago Time)
  • Full Backup & Restart: Daily at 1:00 AM (Chicago Time)
