Homestead v1.3.7 | Minecraft 1.20.1

Follow these steps to update your Homestead mod pack and join the server!

Step 1: Download the Mods

You have two options for updating your mods:

Option A: Extra Mods Only (Recommended)

If you already have the Homestead pack installed, just grab the extra mods we've added.

Download extra-mods.zip

Extract this zip to get the extra .jar files ready.

Option B: Full Mod Folder

If you prefer to start fresh, you can download the entire server mod folder from OneDrive. Delete all the mods in your current mods folder, open the link below, click "Download" at the top to get them all as a zip, and extract them into your mods folder. If you need help feel free to reach out to me.

Open OneDrive Mod Folder

Step 2: Open your Modpack Folder

1. Open CurseForge

Find the Homestead - A Cozy Survival Experience modpack in your CurseForge library. Click on it to open its profile.

2. Open the Options Menu

Right next to the big orange Play button, click the three vertical dots to open the options menu.

3. Open Folder

From the dropdown menu, select Open Folder. This will open the folder containing all your modpack files.

Step 3: Install the Mods

4. Find the 'mods' folder

In the folder that just opened, look for a folder named mods and double-click to open it.

5. Remove Old CreativeCore

Before adding the new mods, find and delete the old CreativeCore `.jar` file from this folder. This prevents confusion and conflicts with the new version you downloaded.

6. Drag and Drop

Finally, drag and drop the `.jar` files you extracted in Step 1 straight into this mods folder. That's it! You can now launch the game.

Step 4: Join the Server!

Launch the game and head to Multiplayer. You can find the server connection address pinned in our Discord server!

Open Discord App
